# 12 · Specific Frequency Claims and the Body

This file audits the individual "magic frequency" numbers people ask about most (432, 528, 639/632, 666, 777 Hz) and the broader claim that specific frequencies map to and "tune" specific organs. It applies the same triangulation protocol as 06 and connects to the physics (09) and clinical evidence (10). Verdicts: **SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / UNSUPPORTED / FABRICATED**.

---

### Two facts that dissolve the mystique

1. **The relationships live in the digits, not the physics.** The famous "3-6-9" patterns (4+3+2=9, 5+2+8=15→6, 6+3+9=18→9) are artifacts of base-10 notation applied to Hz — a unit tied to the arbitrary human *second*. Measure the same tones in cycles per *minute* and every "sacred" number changes. Genuine acoustic relationships are **ratios** (2:1 octave, 3:2 fifth) and survive any unit change; the Greeks and Indian śruti theory worked in ratios. **Every Hz-numerology system is necessarily post-1832** (when Hz-style frequency measurement became possible — see 03 §3.7, 06 §1).
2. **Almost nobody can hear the difference blind.** Absolute pitch is ~1-in-10,000. Blind, 432 vs 440 Hz (~3.6%, a third of a semitone) is indistinguishable to most; 528/639/666/777 are simply *different notes*. Yet reactions to *labeled* audio are strong. That gap — can't identify blind, reacts strongly when labeled — is the fingerprint of **meaning**, not waveform.

## 12.2 So why do people genuinely react?

Real reactions to these tracks are real — driven by mechanisms that are all pitch-independent:

- **Expectation (placebo/nocebo)** — the dominant force. Believing a tone heals produces measurable relaxation; believing 440 Hz is "weaponized" produces measurable discomfort. Largely operates below awareness (this is the honest core of the "subliminal" question).
- **Musical packaging** — "432 Hz healing" tracks are almost always slow, soft, harmonically simple ambient music. Low-arousal music lowers heart rate and cortisol *at any tuning* (10 §10.2). The tuning gets credit for the arrangement's work.
- **Conditioning** — any tone used repeatedly during rest becomes a learned relaxation cue; the nervous system then responds automatically. Real and powerful — and frequency-agnostic (would work with 445 Hz or a fan hum).
- **Symbolic priming** — 666 and 777 work *purely* this way: cultural loading fires the association the instant you know what you're hearing. A real subconscious reaction to a *number*, delivered via sound.

**Why this cannot be literally true:** the chakra system is a *symbolic/subtle-body* map from tantric tradition, not an anatomical one — it has no fixed correspondence to physical organs in its own primary sources (the older texts also disagree on the *number* of chakras). The specific Hz values are the modern solfeggio invention layered on top. There is no mechanism by which an airborne acoustic tone selectively drives one internal organ: sound entering the body is not frequency-routed to organs, and audible frequencies (hundreds of Hz) have wavelengths of ~0.5–1.5 m and do not form organ-selective standing waves in soft tissue.

**Verdict: FABRICATED** as an organ-tuning system.

## 12.4 Where genuine below-awareness sound effects actually live

There *are* documented subconscious effects of sound — just not at the numerology frequencies:

- **Infrasound ~17–19 Hz** — Wiseman's covert-infrasound concert experiment increased chills/unease/"presence"; listeners couldn't hear it (04 §4.7). The closest thing to a real "subliminal frequency effect" — and it's about *very low* frequency coupling with the body, not a magic number.
- **Vibrotactile coupling ~20–120 Hz** — loud low tones are physically *felt* in chest and skull; chant in a resonant chamber literally, subtly shakes you below the "I am being shaken" threshold.
- **Rhythm/tempo entrainment** — heart rate, breathing, and movement track a *beat* (≈1 Hz), the mechanism behind drumming and paced chant (03 §3.6, 10 §10.4).
- **Failed claim — hidden verbal messages:** early-1990s subliminal self-help tape studies (Greenwald et al.) found tape *content* did nothing; what people *believed* was on the tape produced real improvement. Same fingerprint: the label, not the signal.

## 12.6 Honest bottom line

Below ~20 Hz and in rhythm, sound has real subconscious effects **through the body**. Above that — in the 432–963 Hz range of the frequency lists — the effects are also real, but they are the psychology of **meaning, expectation, arrangement, and conditioning** riding on an ordinary musical note. That is not a boring conclusion: it means the effective instrument is not the tone generator but the listener. If you want to *use* sound deliberately (relaxation, focus, ritual), the levers that actually work are tempo, breath pacing, volume/vibration, repetition/conditioning, and framing — none of which require a specific Hertz value.

**Safety note:** this file is descriptive, not medical advice. No frequency treats disease; anyone with a medical condition should not substitute "frequency therapy" for care. Infrasound and high-intensity low-frequency exposure can cause real discomfort and should not be self-administered at volume.
