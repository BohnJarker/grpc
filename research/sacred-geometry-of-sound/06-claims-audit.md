# 06 · Claims Audit: Triangulating the Famous Frequency Claims

Each claim below is audited per the source-triangulation protocol: mainstream/peer-reviewed position, primary documents where they exist, the fringe claim, and an explicit divergence note. Verdicts: **SUPPORTED / PARTIAL / UNSUPPORTED / FABRICATED**.

---

## §1 — "432 Hz is the natural/sacred tuning; A=440 is wrong or harmful"

- **The claim** `[FRINGE]`: A4=432 Hz aligns with nature/the cosmos/the body; 440 Hz was imposed (variants blame Goebbels or a conspiracy) and causes disharmony.
- **Primary record** `[PRIMARY]`: Historical pitch varied enormously — surviving organs and tuning forks range from roughly A≈400 to A≈460+ (Baroque "chamber pitch" ≈415, French diapason normal of 1859 = 435 by law, Verdi's 1884 advocacy of 432 was for *singers' vocal strain*, not cosmology). A440 was standardized via a 1939 international conference and later **ISO 16 (1975)** — a pragmatic compromise, not an occult act.
- **Modern origin of the mystique**: heavily promoted from the 1980s by the **Schiller Institute / Lyndon LaRouche movement** ("Verdi tuning" campaign) and later New Age media. `[MAINSTREAM]` documentation of this provenance.
- **Experimental evidence**: a handful of small studies (e.g., Calamassi & Pomponi 2019, ~30 subjects, 432 vs 440 Hz music) report slightly lower heart rate — small samples, weak blinding, no replication at scale. Nothing supports intrinsic cosmic significance; note that 8 Hz of tuning offset is barely a third of a semitone.
- **The killer premise problem**: Hertz is an arbitrary human unit (cycles per **second**; the second is a human convention). Ancient traditions specified **ratios**, never absolute Hz (see 03 §3.7). Any doctrine of a sacred *number of Hertz* is necessarily post-1832 (when Hz-style frequency measurement became possible).
- **Verdict: UNSUPPORTED** (as metaphysics); the historical-pitch facts are real and interesting on their own.
- **Divergence note**: fringe sources treat Verdi's ergonomic preference and the messy standardization history as evidence of suppressed sacred knowledge; the primary record shows only practical musicians arguing about singers' throats and instrument compatibility.

## §2 — "The ancient Solfeggio frequencies (396/417/528/639/741/852 Hz)"

- **The claim** `[FRINGE]`: six frequencies used in ancient Gregorian chant, suppressed by the Church; 528 Hz "repairs DNA."
- **Actual provenance** `[MAINSTREAM]`: invented in the 1990s by **Joseph Puleo** (with Leonard Horowitz, *Healing Codes for the Biological Apocalypse*, 1999), derived by applying numerology to verse numbers in the Book of Numbers. The real Guidonian solfège (ut-re-mi, from the hymn *Ut queant laxis* `[PRIMARY]`) names **relative scale degrees**, not frequencies; medieval music had no fixed pitch standard at all.
- **DNA-repair claim**: no peer-reviewed evidence; the cited support traces to Horowitz's own publications and misread water/DNA studies.
- **Verdict: FABRICATED** (provenance is fully documented and modern).
- **Divergence note**: this is the audit's cleanest case — the fringe claim's own primary source (Horowitz's book) *states* the numerological derivation; the "ancient" framing is contradicted by the claim's own literature.

## §3 — "110 Hz is the sacred frequency of ancient temples / switches off the language brain"

- **Measured basis** `[MAINSTREAM]`: real — Jahn et al. (1996) found 95–120 Hz resonances in Neolithic chambers; the Hypogeum's Oracle Room resonates near 110 Hz (see 02 §§2.2–2.3).
- **The EEG study**: Cook, Pajot & Leuchter (2008, *Time and Mind*), ~30 subjects listening to tones: at 110 Hz, a shift in prefrontal asymmetry and relatively deactivated left-temporal (language-associated) activity vs. neighboring frequencies. **Pilot-scale, never independently replicated**; the authors themselves framed it as preliminary.
- **The fringe version** `[FRINGE]`: "ancients tuned temples to 110 Hz to induce trance / deactivate language centers" — stated as established.
- **Verdict: PARTIAL.** Resonances: measured. Deliberate tuning: unproven (chamber size explains the frequency band). Neurological effect: one unreplicated pilot. This is the project's highest-value open experiment — see 07 §7.4.

## §4 — "Cymatics proves sound creates form/matter" & "sacred geometry appears in sound"

- **Real physics** `[MAINSTREAM]`: Chladni patterns (Ernst Chladni, 1787 `[PRIMARY]`) — sand on a vibrating plate collects at nodal lines, producing beautiful symmetric figures; Faraday waves do similar in liquids. Fully explained by standing-wave mechanics; pattern geometry is a property of **the plate** (shape, material, mode), not of a "sacred" frequency.
- **The fringe extension** `[FRINGE]`: Hans Jenny (*Kymatik*, 1967) coined "cymatics" and drifted from observation toward vibration-as-creative-principle; successors claim sound "structures" water or matter generally, sometimes citing Masaru Emoto's water-crystal photographs — which had no blinding or controls and failed under double-blind conditions `[MAINSTREAM]`.
- **Verdict: PARTIAL** — the phenomenon is real and genuinely is "geometry made audible" (a legitimate, beautiful teaching tool for this project); the metaphysical extension is UNSUPPORTED.
- **Divergence note**: divergence begins exactly where sources stop describing standing waves and start using the word "creation."

## §5 — "Schumann resonance (7.83 Hz) is Earth's heartbeat / the brain's natural tuning"

- **Real physics** `[MAINSTREAM]`: the Earth-ionosphere cavity resonates at ~7.83 Hz (+ harmonics), predicted by W.O. Schumann (1952), routinely measured. It is an **electromagnetic** resonance, not an acoustic one, with field strengths at the surface that are orders of magnitude below the electromagnetic noise of any inhabited building.
- **The fringe version** `[FRINGE]`: because alpha-band EEG (~8–12 Hz) numerically neighbors 7.83 Hz, claims of tuning/entrainment between Earth and brain; "rising Schumann frequency = human consciousness shift" (the resonance's base mode is not rising; daily/seasonal variation is well characterized).
- **Verdict: UNSUPPORTED** (as a consciousness mechanism). Numerical coincidence between unrelated oscillators — an EM cavity mode and an electrochemical neural rhythm — carries no causal weight.

## §6 — "The Great Pyramid / megaliths are frequency machines (power plants, healing chambers)"

- **Measured basis** `[MAINSTREAM]`: pyramid chambers, like all hard-walled rooms, have resonances (the King's Chamber and its granite coffer audibly ring; an oft-cited coffer resonance sits near ~110–120 Hz — consistent with its dimensions). A 2018 *Journal of Applied Physics* paper modeling EM resonance in an idealized pyramid was press-hyped far beyond its content (a theoretical scattering calculation, not a discovery of function).
- **The fringe version** `[FRINGE]`: Christopher Dunn's "Giza power plant," healing-chamber tourism, "pyramid energy."
- **Verdict: UNSUPPORTED** for any machine/power/healing function; SUPPORTED only for "stone chambers resonate," which is true of every stone chamber including a highway underpass.
- **Divergence note**: the fringe pattern here is *function inflation* — from "has a resonance" (physics, trivially true) to "is a resonator built to do something" (archaeology, requires evidence of use) to "channels energy" (physics-flavored metaphysics, unfalsifiable).

---

## Meta-pattern across all six audits

1. **A real measured kernel** (resonance, pattern, historical fact) —
2. **stripped of its ordinary explanation** (room size sets frequency; standardization politics; numerology in the primary source) —
3. **inflated into a universal doctrine** stated in modern units (Hz) that ancient cultures did not possess —
4. **sustained by partial citation** of mainstream sources (Stargate minus its conclusion; Jahn et al. minus the geometry explanation).

The audit protocol that catches all four moves is simply: *read the primary document to the end, and ask what the geometry alone would predict.*
