# 07 · Methodology: How to Actually Run This Research

A concrete, ethics-aware program in three tracks: **field acoustics**, **human-subjects psychoacoustics**, and **historical/textual triangulation**. Designed so every hypothesis in 01 has a falsifiable test.

---

## 7.1 Track A — Field measurement of site acoustics

**Goal:** replicate and extend the measurements in 02 with a uniform, open protocol so sites become comparable.

**Core measurements per site** (all standard room acoustics, per ISO 3382 adapted to heritage settings):
1. **Impulse responses** (exponential sine sweep from an omnidirectional source; balloon pops as backup where electronics are restricted) at a grid of source/receiver positions → derive reverberation time (T20/T30), clarity (C50/C80), and early decay time.
2. **Modal analysis:** low-frequency sweep 40–250 Hz to map standing-wave frequencies and node/antinode positions; compare measured modes against predictions from measured chamber geometry (this directly tests "tuned vs. just room-sized" — the 02 §2.3 divergence).
3. **Infrasound survey:** calibrated measurement microphone (flat to <10 Hz) during (a) ambient conditions, (b) wind, (c) reconstructed sound sources (drum, horn replica, voice) — tests the 04 §4.7 conjecture.
4. **Auralization:** publish measured impulse responses openly so any laboratory can convolve chant/instruments with real site acoustics — this is what makes Track B possible without occupying the site.

**Heritage & community ethics:** written permission from site authorities; non-contact methods only; no measurement during living ritual use without custodian collaboration; indigenous sites only as partnered projects with cultural custodians as co-authors.

**Intentionality test (H1), stated falsifiably:** builders "exploited acoustics" predicts that, across a site sample, *ritual-featured* chambers (art, deposits, formal architecture) show stronger/cleaner vocal-range resonance than comparable *non-ritual* chambers (quarries, storage, natural voids of similar size), controlling for volume and material. No difference → H1 fails.

## 7.2 Track B — Human-subjects psychoacoustics (lab)

**Design philosophy:** everything preregistered; sham conditions everywhere; expectation measured, not ignored — because expectation is probably half the phenomenon and *that is itself a finding*.

**Experiment B1 — Does the room's acoustic alone alter state?**
- Participants hear identical chant/drone recordings convolved with (i) measured ancient-chamber impulse response, (ii) acoustically matched neutral room, (iii) anechoic. Blind to condition, fixed loudness.
- Measures: validated awe scale, absorption, mood (PANAS), heart rate/HRV, EEG spectral power.
- Prediction (H2): chamber condition > neutral > anechoic on awe/absorption.

**Experiment B2 — The 110 Hz replication (highest value, cheapest)**
- Direct, adequately powered (n ≥ 100, vs. Cook et al.'s ~30), preregistered replication of the 2008 EEG finding: tones at 90/110/130 Hz, within-subject, order counterbalanced, analysts blind.
- Any credible outcome is publishable: replication would be the first solid neuro-effect in archaeoacoustics; failure retires the field's most-cited fringe anchor (06 §3).

**Experiment B3 — Frequency-label placebo (the 432 Hz test)**
- 2×2: music actually at 432 vs. 440 Hz × *told* "healing 432" vs. "standard 440." If reported benefit follows the **label** rather than the tuning, the 432 phenomenon is expectation — the design cleanly separates acoustics from belief.

**Experiment B4 — Geometry without sound**
- VR presentation of chamber/nave/kiva geometries (vs. matched-volume neutral boxes) in silence vs. with matched audio; tests how much of the "sacred space" response is visual geometry (04), acoustics (02), or their interaction.

## 7.3 Track C — Historical and textual triangulation

- For each tradition in 03: assemble primary sources on sound practice (Upaniṣads, tajwīd manuals, cantillation treatises, Boethius, liturgical rubrics) with dates and manuscript provenance.
- For each fringe claim in 06: trace the **first appearance** of the claim in print (the solfeggio audit shows how decisive this is — a "lost ancient knowledge" claim with a 1999 first attestation is self-refuting).
- Maintain the tagged bibliography (08) as the living output.

## 7.4 Analysis standards (apply to all tracks)

- Preregistration (OSF) for every confirmatory study; exploratory analyses labeled as such.
- Effect sizes with confidence intervals, not bare p-values; Bayesian model comparison where priors are contested (psi claims get the Reber-Alcock low prior, stated openly).
- Adversarial collaboration invited for any contested result (the Hyman-Utts model from 05 §5.2 — used properly this time: agree on protocol *before* data).
- All impulse responses, EEG data, and analysis code published openly.

## 7.5 Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Site damage / community offense | Non-invasive protocol, custodial partnership, permits |
| Infrasound discomfort in B-track | Levels within occupational exposure guidance; screening; stop-rule |
| Expectation contamination | Blinding, sham conditions, expectation measured as covariate |
| Cherry-picked site sample recreating the 110 Hz myth | Pre-registered site inclusion criteria incl. non-ritual control chambers |
| Researcher belief drift (both directions) | Adversarial collaboration; skeptic and sympathist co-analysis |

## 7.6 Falsification ledger (what would change our minds)

- **H1 falls** if ritual chambers show no acoustic distinction from matched non-ritual voids.
- **H2 falls** if B1/B4 show no state difference beyond expectation effects.
- **H4 (anti-psi null) falls** if a preregistered, multi-lab, adversarially designed psi experiment produces a replicated effect — the stance in 05 is a posterior, not a dogma.
- **The 110 Hz claim dies or graduates** on B2's outcome; the project commits to publishing either result.
