## 13.1 What the source actually is

Robert Lawrence Kuhn (creator/host of *Closer to Truth*) catalogued **325+ live theories of consciousness** and organized them into roughly ten families without adjudicating between them:

| Family | Core commitment |
|---|---|
| Materialism (many subtypes) | Consciousness is brain activity — identity, functionalism, illusionism, predictive processing, global workspace, higher-order theories |
| Non-reductive physicalism | Physical, but not reducible to neural description |
| Quantum theories | Consciousness tied to quantum processes (e.g., Orch-OR) |
| Integrated Information Theory | Consciousness = integrated information (Φ) |
| Panpsychisms | Experience is a fundamental property of matter |
| Monisms | Mind and matter as aspects of one underlying reality |
| Dualisms | Mind distinct from matter (substance/property variants) |
| Idealisms | Consciousness is fundamental; matter derivative |
| Anomalous/challenge theories | Theories motivated by contested phenomena (psi, NDEs) |
| Agnostic/mysterian | The problem may exceed human cognition |

The honest headline: **consciousness science is pre-paradigmatic.** 325+ live theories means no consensus exists on what consciousness *is* — a rare, documented case of a mature scientific field with the metaphysics still fully open.

## 13.2 Why this belongs in this project

Every strong claim this project audits — "sound expands consciousness," "frequency *is* consciousness," "chant induces higher states," "psionics" — quietly presupposes an answer to the question Kuhn shows is unanswered. The landscape gives us a precise way to say what this project can and cannot claim:

**What is theory-neutral (and therefore safe ground):** sound demonstrably alters **states** of consciousness — arousal, absorption, trance, awe, the "small self," sensory-deprivation imagery (files 04, 05 §5.4, 10). These are measurable under *any* of the 325 theories; a materialist, a panpsychist, and an idealist all have to accommodate the same EEG, HRV, and phenomenology data. Our Track B experiments (07) live entirely on this ground.

**What is a metaphysical bet (and must be labeled as such):** any claim that sound-induced states *reveal* the nature of consciousness — e.g., "the chamber connects you to universal mind" (idealist bet) or, equally, "it's *just* neurons misfiring" (materialist bet). The landscape cuts both ways: fringe enthusiasm and reflexive debunking are **both** unearned metaphysical confidence. The project's stance stays agnostic about what consciousness is while being rigorous about what sound does to it.

## 13.3 The precise upgrade this gives our claims audit

File 06's fringe-inflation pipeline gains a fifth step worth naming: fringe material often **borrows the openness of consciousness science as if it were evidence** — "scientists can't explain consciousness, therefore frequency healing." The landscape shows why this fails: 325 competing theories is an argument for *humility*, not for any particular exotic claim. An open question is not an open door.

Symmetrically, it disciplines the skeptical side: 05's rejection of psionics rests on *replication failure* (the right ground), not on "materialism is true" (which would be betting on one family of the landscape).

## 13.4 Divergence note (triangulation per protocol)

- **Kuhn's paper** `[MAINSTREAM]`: deliberately neutral taxonomy; Kuhn declines to rank theories in print.
- **Essentia Foundation** (publisher of the video): an advocacy platform for **analytic idealism** (Bernardo Kastrup). The interview format invites Kuhn to voice leanings his paper withholds, within a platform whose editorial mission favors consciousness-as-fundamental. Nothing wrong with that — but per the tagging rule, the *paper* is the citable object; the *video's framing* is a perspective, not a finding.
- An interactive companion, the "Consciousness Atlas" web app, visualizes the taxonomy and is useful as a map, not as evidence for any node on it.
