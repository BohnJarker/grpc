# 00 · A Learning Path Through Sound

A self-study curriculum built on top of this project. Each stage lists: the core ideas, where to read (project files + one outside source), and **recall questions** — try to answer them from memory *before* rereading; that retrieval effort is what makes learning stick.

---

## Stage 1 — What sound *is* (physics foundations)

**Core ideas:** sound = pressure waves in a medium; frequency (Hz) vs. pitch, amplitude vs. loudness (dB), wavelength λ = c/f (343 m/s in air); harmonics and the overtone series; why a note contains many frequencies at once (timbre = spectrum).

**Read:** file 09 §9.1–9.2 · outside: any intro acoustics text or *The Sound Book* (Trevor Cox — the Stonehenge researcher; the best popular acoustics book).

**Recall questions:**
1. A 110 Hz tone in air — what's its wavelength? (Work it out: 343/110.)
2. Why do a flute and a violin playing the same note sound different?
3. What physically doubles when a pitch rises one octave?

## Stage 2 — Resonance and rooms (the geometry link)

**Core ideas:** standing waves; room modes f = c/2L; Q factor and why stone rooms ring; Sabine reverberation RT60 ≈ 0.161·V/A; Helmholtz resonators; waveguides; focusing (domes, whispering galleries).

**Read:** file 09 in full — it was written to be this stage's textbook chapter.

**Recall questions:**
1. Why does a 3 m stone chamber "prefer" ~57 and ~114 Hz? Derive it.
2. Why can't a wooden hall reproduce the Hypogeum effect at any size?
3. A cathedral and a closet have the same RT60 formula — which variable makes the cathedral reverberant, and which keeps the closet dead?

## Stage 3 — Psychoacoustics (ear and brain)

**Core ideas:** hearing range 20 Hz–20 kHz and why sensitivity peaks 2–5 kHz (speech); localization via interaural time/level differences and why reverberation defeats it (precedence effect); masking; missing fundamental; beats; vibrotactile hearing below ~100 Hz; infrasound effects.

**Read:** files 09 §9.5 and 04 §4.7 · outside: any psychoacoustics primer (e.g., Moore, *An Introduction to the Psychology of Hearing* — the standard text).

**Recall questions:**
1. Why does a voice in a resonant chamber seem to come from "everywhere"?
2. You hear a pitch at 100 Hz from a phone speaker that can't physically produce 100 Hz. What's happening?
3. Two chanters drone a near-unison. What new, slow phenomenon appears, and in what frequency band?

## Stage 4 — Sound and the body/mind

**Core ideas:** what music/sound demonstrably does (anxiety, mood, memory — and through which mechanisms: breath pacing, expectation, absorption, social synchrony); the evidence gradient from music therapy (strong) to frequency-numerology (none); awe and architectural psychology.

**Read:** files 10 and 04 · then 06 to see the claims that fail.

**Recall questions:**
1. Name three *pitch-independent* mechanisms by which chanting calms the body.
2. What makes the 40 Hz Alzheimer's research methodologically different from 432 Hz claims?
3. What two ingredients define awe, and what does awe do to the sense of self?

## Stage 5 — The ancient world's acoustics (the payoff)

**Core ideas:** now the site catalog reads differently — every "mystery" in files 02 and 11 should resolve into a Stage 2 mechanism plus a Stage 3–4 percept. Test yourself: Chichén Itzá = periodic reflection → chirp; Hypogeum = room mode + vibrotactile chant; Chavín = waveguide network + localization failure; bianzhong = engineered two-mode vibration.

**Read:** files 02, 11, 03 — in that order.

**Recall questions:**
1. For each site above, name the physical mechanism *and* the perceptual effect it exploits.
2. Why is Chavín the strongest case for intentional design? (Four converging evidence types.)
3. Why do human-scale stone rooms worldwide resonate near the male chanting voice — coincidence, conspiracy, or geometry?

## Stage 6 — Thinking critically about sound claims

**Core ideas:** the four-step fringe inflation pattern (06 meta-pattern); ratio-mysticism (ancient, real) vs. Hertz-mysticism (necessarily modern); how to check a claim's *first attestation*; what a real frequency-specific effect looks like when proven properly.

**Read:** files 06, 05, then 07 §7.6 (falsification ledger).

**Recall questions:**
1. Why is any doctrine about a sacred *number of Hertz* necessarily post-1832?
2. The solfeggio frequencies claim can be refuted from its own primary source — how?
3. Design (verbally) an experiment separating a frequency's acoustic effect from its label's placebo effect.

---

## How to use this

- One stage at a time; answer the recall questions from memory first, check, move on only when you can teach the stage's ideas to someone else.
- Interleave: when on Stage 4, re-answer one Stage 2 question cold — spacing beats cramming.
- The single most load-bearing idea in the whole curriculum is Stage 2's **f = c/2L**: geometry sets frequency. Everything sacred, spooky, and commercial in this field is downstream of whether one understands that equation.
